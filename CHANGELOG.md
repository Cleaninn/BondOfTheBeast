# Bond of the Beast 2.0.0

Minecraft 1.20.1 / Fabric

- Reworked voluntary contracts and forced taming, with stage-based commands, resistance and permanent bonds.
- Removed bond leveling and legacy combat buffs; expanded restrictions, armor management and item forwarding.
- Redesigned the Grimoire and Pet Diary, with 3D portraits, clearer controls and live pet status.
- Improved collars, rendered leashes, pet beds, respawn rules and the sit command.
- Added familiar fox integration, protective accessories, an energy source ring and catalyst cookies.
- Updated item textures and added visible eating effects and sounds when feeding pets.
