# Vanilla ability icons

Mode: built-in image_gen, one transparent 5x2 sprite atlas.
The selected atlas was split into ten cells and resized to native 16x16 PNG textures with nearest-neighbor sampling.
Assets: src/main/resources/assets/bondofthebeast/textures/gui/abilities/vanilla/.
The older generated icons and original generation output remain preserved.

## Cleanup prompt (applied to the generated atlas)

Edit this transparent 5x2 Minecraft pixel-art ability sprite atlas. Preserve EXACTLY the ten existing subjects, their reading order, simple vanilla colors and overall 5:2 grid layout. Remove ALL stray isolated pixels, specks, scraps and translucent fringes anywhere in the transparent padding outside the ten main sprites. Each cell must contain exactly one compact clean sprite centered with at least 15 percent completely transparent padding on every side. Keep the prohibition minus attached to its tool or hand; keep fangs attached next to the blood droplet. No unattached extra floating squares except the intended four rays directly around the aura paw. Make shapes very coarse and solid, ready to be reduced to 16x16 pixels per cell. Crisp nearest-neighbor pixel art, dark outlines, no blur, no gray background, no typography. Keep original simple unadorned Minecraft style; this is only cleaning and simplifying the sprite sheet.

## Generation prompt

ONE sprite sheet for Minecraft Java mod GUI ability icons. Transparent background. EXACT 5 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, landscape 5:2 overall shape. All ten cells separate, no panel backgrounds, no lettering, no numerals, no labels. Each icon is extremely simple TRUE 16x16 logical pixel art, enlarged by nearest neighbor only, square flat pixels, maximum 5 solid colors per icon, thick readable silhouette, 1 pixel dark outline, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no lighting bloom, no realistic details, no ornate trim, no decorative flourishes. Match the plain vanilla Minecraft inventory texture language: stone gray, leather brown, dull red, desaturated green and cream. Generously blank transparent margin around every centered icon. Reading order: top row 1 seated gray wolf side profile with two triangular ears and folded hind leg, 2 simple teal paw with small curved return arrow, 3 plain iron-gray shield with very simple small paw mark, 4 simple cream paw with four dull green aura rays, 5 single dark red blood droplet with two small cream fangs. Bottom row 6 simple gray pickaxe with small red minus mark, 7 simple tan hand with small red minus mark, 8 plain gray sword with small red minus mark, 9 plain brown closed grimoire with one small teal spiral, 10 plain leather-brown chestplate with small gray padlock. They must resemble modest hand-drawn vanilla 16 pixel item textures, not polished fantasy icons. NO GOLD, NO JEWELS, NO EMBROIDERY. Use only large chunky pixel groups suitable for direct downsampling to 16x16.
