# Ability icon atlas

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool, transparent background.
The selected 5x2 atlas was split into ten cells and resized to 24x24 using nearest-neighbor sampling. Original generated atlas preserved under Codex generated_images.
Final assets: BondOfTheBeast/src/main/resources/assets/bondofthebeast/textures/gui/abilities/*.png.

## Prompt

Create ONE cohesive Minecraft mod ability-icon sprite atlas on transparent background. Exact 5 columns by 2 rows, ten equally sized square cells, generously transparent padding around each icon, no grid lines, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no background panels. Each icon centered within its cell, completely separated from neighbours. Pixel art with crisp square pixels, small readable 24x24-style item sprites, warm ochre gold highlights, burgundy and dark sepia outlines, parchment cream accents, very limited consistent palette matching an antique red leather grimoire. No smooth shading, no realistic rendering. Reading order top row: (1) seated dog silhouette with curled tail, (2) golden paw inside curved return arrow, (3) shield with paw emblem, (4) paw surrounded by small radiant sparks, (5) two vampire fangs above red droplet. Bottom row: (6) pickaxe with small prohibition cross, (7) hand with small prohibition cross, (8) sheathed sword tied with ribbon for pacifism, (9) paw dissolving into a spiral vortex, (10) chestplate with small padlock. Keep icons visually distinctive and consistently scaled. Entire atlas landscape aspect ratio 5:2.
